## 中文

我有创业与业务实践背景，正在探索如何用 AI-native 的方式，重新构建产品、研究与日常运营中的工作流。

过去的创业经历让我更关注真实问题：用户是否愿意使用，流程是否能跑通，结果是否可验证，以及一件事能否从“靠人硬扛”变成可持续运转的系统。

现在，我正在把这些经验转化为 AI 时代的新能力与新项目。

### 我正在做什么

- 用 AI 与 Agent 重构信息收集、研究分析和日常运营流程
- 探索 AI-native 产品：从真实需求到可验证的 MVP
- 将创业中的业务判断、用户视角与执行经验，转化为可复用的方法和工具
- 持续研究 AI 产品、行业变化与技术带来的新机会

### 我关心的问题

- AI 能替代哪些重复劳动，又在哪些环节必须保留人的判断？
- 一个 AI 产品如何从“看起来很酷”走向真正解决问题？
- 创业者如何借助 AI，以更小的团队和成本构建新业务？
- 如何让研究、决策与执行形成可复盘、可迭代的系统？

### 这里会持续更新

- AI-native 产品与工作流实验
- Agent / 自动化实践
- 创业经验在 AI 时代的重新应用
- 研究笔记、项目复盘与可复用工具

> 我相信，AI 的价值不止在于提高效率，更在于可以重新定义价值创造的方式。

## English

I come from an entrepreneurial and business-practice background, and I am exploring how to rebuild product, research, and operational workflows in an AI-native way.

My experience in entrepreneurship has made me care about real problems: whether people will use a product, whether a workflow can run end to end, whether outcomes are verifiable, and whether a process can evolve from relying on individual effort into a sustainable system.

I am now translating that experience into new capabilities and projects for the AI era.

### What I'm working on

- Rebuilding information gathering, research, and operational workflows with AI and agents
- Exploring AI-native products, from real needs to testable MVPs
- Turning entrepreneurial judgment, user perspective, and execution experience into reusable methods and tools
- Researching AI products, industry shifts, and new opportunities created by technology

### Questions I care about

- Which repetitive tasks can AI take on, and where must human judgment remain?
- How can an AI product move from looking impressive to solving a real problem?
- How can founders use AI to build new businesses with smaller teams and lower costs?
- How can research, decisions, and execution become a system that is reviewable and continuously improved?

### What you'll find here

- AI-native product and workflow experiments
- Agent and automation practices
- Reapplying entrepreneurial experience in the AI era
- Research notes, project retrospectives, and reusable tools

> I believe AI's value goes beyond improving efficiency; it can redefine how value is created.
